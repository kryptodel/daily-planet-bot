Privacy Policy

Terms of Service

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Daily Planet Bot collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

The bot may collect only the information necessary to operate correctly, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Slash command interactions
  • Configuration data required for server features

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Passwords
  • Email addresses
  • Payment information
  • Private Discord messages
  • Personal files or media

How Your Data Is Used

The collected information is used exclusively to provide the bot's features, respond to commands, maintain server settings, and improve reliability.

Data Sharing

Daily Planet Bot never sells, rents, or shares collected data with advertisers or third parties. Information may only be disclosed if legally required.

Data Security

Reasonable security measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Data Removal

Server owners may request removal of stored configuration data by contacting the developer.

Children's Privacy

The bot is not intended to knowingly collect information from children outside of data provided by Discord itself.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official Daily Planet Discord server.
